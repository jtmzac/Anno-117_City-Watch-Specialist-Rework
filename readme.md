# City Watch Specialist Rework
New v1.1.0 Update: Added legendary variants from specialists research including the ones for amenities and all city watches.

Changes city watch specialists to affect all of their respective building types on the island and gives each building a small area buff of the relevant type (fire safety/health/happiness).

In addition to the small buff, rare, epic and legendary specialists all add +1 patrol unit each.

Common, rare and epic specialists are obtained from traders.

Legendary specialists are obtained from research. Economy has the all city watches and health specialists, Civic has the amenities and happiness specialists, and Military has the fire specialist.

The buff values may be wildy unbalanced depending on difficulty settings and other factors such as city status debuffs and building density. I've tried to set default values that are on the lower side to prevent them being too ridiculous.

## Fire Safety/Health/Happiness Buff Values for Each Rarity
| Rarity     | Default |
| -------    | -------   |
| Common     | 0.3   |
| Rare       | 0.6   |
| Epic       | 1.0    |
| Legendary  | 1.5    |

## The All City Watches and Amenities Specialists
These have been changes a bit differently since they target all 3 of their building types but still affect all of their buildings on the island.

The all city watches buff gives -25% maintenance cost and an area buff of +1 money.

The amenities buff gives -20% maintenance cost, a +1 belief area buff, and also an area buff of +0.2 of fire safety, happiness and health.

## Compatibility with Specialist Pools Rebalance
If you use my other mod that removes the common, rare and epic specialists from traders by default (Specialist Pools Rebalance) then they will be re-added to the pool at the non-Roman trader in Albion (Manx)

## Customisation
If you do not like the provided values then to change them please edit the appropriate sections in the mods assets.xml.

You may need to remove and re-add the specialists to your officium/villa to apply new values
